### c Draft three personas, that is, abstractions of representative groups of stakeholders (users, developers, or similar) interacting with the system. The idea of a persona here is the one from agile requirements analysis.

### d Three members of your group will each impersonate one persona and formulate two competency questions each, so your group has six competency questions. This is much fewer than usual in such processes; it will allow you to fulfill all requirements and still have a small ontology.

#### Chemist
- Which measurements have less than 10% uncertanty?
- What is the given measurments uncertanty?
#### Engineer
- What is the smallest possible value an object can have, given its uncertanty?
- What is the smallest value of uncertanty an object can have given certain circumstances?

#### Student
- what are the values to the properties to the objects?
- which unit is the measure of the property given for a given object?
